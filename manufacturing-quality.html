TABLET MANUFACTURING

Attention to the manufacturing process.

Pharmaceutical tablet manufacturing involves coordinated activities that may include material handling, preparation, processing, compression, coating where applicable, packaging and product checks. The specific operations depend on the formulation, equipment and approved process for each product.

KEY PROCESS AREAS

A structured approach to manufacturing

The following areas are relevant to pharmaceutical manufacturing. The company's specific activities should be confirmed before describing them as established capabilities.

01

Material Management

Appropriate identification, handling and documentation of materials according to the relevant procedures.

02

Production Operations

Controlled manufacturing activities appropriate to the approved formulation and product process.

03

In-Process Checks

Process monitoring and checks as required by the applicable product procedures and specifications.

04

Packaging Operations

Product presentation and packaging activities appropriate to the product and its approved requirements.

05

Documentation

Manufacturing records and supporting documentation maintained in accordance with applicable requirements.

06

Process Improvement

Reviewing processes and identifying opportunities to strengthen consistency and operational effectiveness.

QUALITY AWARENESS

Quality is part of the process.

Quality-related responsibilities are important throughout the pharmaceutical product lifecycle.

Process consistency

Defined procedures, appropriate training and process documentation support consistent operations when implemented effectively.

Product requirements

Product specifications, testing requirements and acceptance criteria must be established and followed according to applicable regulations and approved procedures.

Documentation and traceability

Suitable records support review of manufacturing activities and help establish the history of a product batch where required.

Regulatory responsibilities

The relevant licences, approvals, standards and regulatory obligations depend on the company's activities and the markets in which it operates.
