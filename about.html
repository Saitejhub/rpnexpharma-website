OUR COMPANY

A developing pharmaceutical manufacturing organisation

RP Nexpharma Private Limited is focused on establishing and developing its own pharmaceutical manufacturing activities. The company's current in-house dosage-form focus is tablets.

Our approach is centred on building appropriate manufacturing capabilities, developing dependable processes and creating professional relationships with business partners.

With an international outlook, RP Nexpharma aims to explore opportunities to serve business partners and markets beyond its immediate operating environment.
